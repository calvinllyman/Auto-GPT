**Yes — in most cases, get pre-approved before serious touring.**

A pre-approval shows sellers you’re a prepared buyer and helps you shop in a realistic range. Without it, it’s easy to waste weekends on homes that won’t finance cleanly.

## Exceptions

Cash buyers and certain unique situations can differ — but even then, proof of funds still matters.
