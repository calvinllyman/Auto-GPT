**Homes for Heroes is built for community heroes — teachers, first responders, medical professionals, military members, and related roles are common categories.**

Exact eligibility follows program rules and confirmation steps. Don’t assume you’re in or out based on a job title alone.

## How savings fit the transaction

Hero Rewards® savings (when you qualify) work alongside a normal buy or sell — they don’t replace pricing strategy, inspections, or financing. Read [How Homes for Heroes savings work](/resources/guides/homes-for-heroes-how-savings-work).
