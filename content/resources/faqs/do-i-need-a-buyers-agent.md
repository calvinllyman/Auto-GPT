**You can legally buy without an agent — but most buyers are better protected with clear representation.**

A buyer’s agent helps you interpret comps, structure offers, navigate inspections, and keep deadlines from slipping. The listing agent’s fiduciary duty is to the seller.

## What good representation looks like

- Local search strategy across Yukon, Mustang, OKC, and beyond
- Offer terms matched to the property and your financing
- Inspection prioritization and negotiation support
- Coordination through title, lenders, and closing
