**It depends on price, condition, presentation, and current buyer demand in your pocket of the metro.**

Some well-priced homes move quickly. Others need strategy adjustments after the first wave of showings. Timeline goals should drive prep and pricing — not the other way around.

## What you can control

- Condition and cleanliness before photos
- Pricing against real comps
- Availability for showings
- Response speed on offers and repairs
