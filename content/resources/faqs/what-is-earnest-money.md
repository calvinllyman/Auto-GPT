**Earnest money is a good-faith deposit you put down when your offer is accepted — it shows the seller you’re serious.**

It’s typically held by a title company or escrow holder (not pocketed by the seller on day one) and is credited toward your cash to close if the deal finishes.

## Can you lose it?

It depends on the contract and why the deal ends. If you terminate within valid contingency rights (for example, during an agreed option/inspection window), you may recover it under the contract terms. If you default outside those rights, the seller may have a claim to it.

## How much is enough?

There’s no single number for every OKC metro home. The right amount signals strength for the price point without stretching you uncomfortably before closing.
