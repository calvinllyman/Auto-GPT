**The best time to list is when your home is ready and your timeline is real — not when a headline says “spring.”**

Seasonality can change traffic, but condition and price usually decide results. A polished home listed in a quieter month can outperform a rushed listing in a busy one.

## Questions that pick the date

- Do you need to close by a hard deadline?
- Is prep (repairs, paint, declutter) finished enough for strong photos?
- Can you accommodate showings for the first 7–14 days?
- Are you buying another home at the same time?
