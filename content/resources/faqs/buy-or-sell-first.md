**There’s no universal answer — the right sequence depends on your equity, risk comfort, financing, and how long you can carry two homes (or temporary housing).**

## Sell first

Often lower financial risk. You know your proceeds before you buy. Tradeoff: you may need temporary housing or a tight closing alignment.

## Buy first

Works when you can qualify and carry overlapping housing costs — or when you find a rare home you can’t risk losing. Tradeoff: more payment pressure if your current home sells slowly.

## A third path

Sometimes a contingent offer, rent-back, or bridge strategy fits. Those tools depend on the specific properties and lender rules.
