The inspection period is where good deals get smarter — or messy. Use it to gather facts, not to renegotiate every cosmetic detail.

## What inspections often cover

- Structure, roof, HVAC, plumbing, and electrical systems at a high level
- Safety items and major deferred maintenance
- Optional extras (sewer scope, radon, pest) depending on the property and your risk comfort

Your inspector’s job is to report. Your job — with your agent — is to prioritize.

## How to prioritize findings

1. Safety and habitability first
2. Expensive systems with short remaining life next
3. Cosmetics last (paint, outdated finishes, minor wear)

Not every finding needs a credit. Some need a repair. Some just need a future maintenance plan.

## Keep communication clean

Respond within your option window. Ask for clarity in writing. Decide whether you’re walking, renegotiating, or proceeding — then move.
