Selling well is a sequence. Skip a step and you usually pay for it in price, time, or stress.

## 1. Valuation and goals

Start with where the home stands against recent comps and what you need from the proceeds and timeline. [Request a home valuation](/sell/home-valuation).

## 2. Prep that buyers will notice

Focus on cleanliness, light, minor repairs, and curb appeal. Use the [seller prep checklist](/resources/checklists/seller-prep-checklist).

## 3. Pricing and presentation

Price for the buyers who are active now. Pair that with strong photos and showing access. See [pricing strategy when the market is shifting](/resources/market-reports/pricing-strategy-shifting-market).

## 4. Offers and negotiation

Compare more than price — financing strength, timelines, contingencies, and repair expectations. A “higher” offer that falls apart isn’t a win.

## 5. Inspection through closing

Stay responsive. Decide quickly on repair requests. Keep title, insurance, and move-out logistics on a shared calendar.

## How long will it take?

It depends on price, condition, and demand in your pocket of the metro. Read [How long does it take to sell?](/resources/faqs/how-long-to-sell).
