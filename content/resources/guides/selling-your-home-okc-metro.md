Selling well is a sequence. Skip a step and you usually pay for it in price, time, or stress.

## 1. Clarify the goal

Are you maximizing net, maximizing speed, or coordinating a same-day purchase? Your goal changes pricing, repairs, and possession strategy. If you are also buying, read [Should I buy or sell first?](/resources/faqs/buy-or-sell-first).

## 2. Valuation with real comps

Start with where the home stands against recent solds that match condition and location — not the highest asking price on Zillow. [Request a home valuation](/sell/home-valuation).

## 3. Prep that buyers will notice

Focus on cleanliness, light, minor repairs, and curb appeal. Use the [seller prep checklist](/resources/checklists/seller-prep-checklist).

## 4. Price for the buyers who show up now

Launch pricing should create interest in week one. Review [pricing strategy](/resources/market-reports/pricing-strategy-shifting-market) and [when to list](/resources/faqs/when-to-list-my-home).

## 5. Marketing that creates real showings

Strong photos, accurate remarks, and easy showing access matter more than gimmicks. See the [marketing page](/sell/marketing) for how Calvin approaches listing exposure.

## 6. Offer review beyond the top line

Compare financing strength, earnest money, timelines, option length, and contingencies. A “higher” offer that collapses is not a win.

## 7. Inspection through closing

Stay responsive. Decide quickly on repair requests using a calm framework: [option period guide](/resources/guides/oklahoma-option-period-explained). Keep title, insurance, and move-out logistics on one calendar. Use the [moving day checklist](/resources/checklists/moving-day-checklist).

## How long will it take?

It depends on price, condition, and demand in your pocket of the metro. Read [How long does it take to sell?](/resources/faqs/how-long-to-sell).

## Selling a rental?

Use [Hold vs. sell a rental](/resources/guides/hold-vs-sell-rental-property-okc) before you assume listing is (or isn’t) the answer.
