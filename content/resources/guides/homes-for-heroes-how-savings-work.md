Homes for Heroes is an affiliation, not a separate brokerage brand. If you qualify as a community hero, you may access Hero Rewards® savings when you buy or sell — while still working directly with Calvin.

## Who often qualifies

Teachers, first responders, medical professionals, military members, and other community heroes are common categories — confirmation depends on program rules.

## How to think about the savings

Treat Hero Rewards as part of your overall transaction plan, not a replacement for pricing strategy, inspections, or financing. The best outcome still comes from a clear process.
