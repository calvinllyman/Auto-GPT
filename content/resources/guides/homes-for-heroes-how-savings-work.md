Homes for Heroes is an affiliation that can provide **Hero Rewards® savings** to eligible community heroes when they buy or sell through a participating affiliate. It is not a separate brokerage brand, and it is not a shortcut around inspections, appraisals, or financing.

Used well, it is a meaningful thank-you layered onto a professionally run transaction.

## Who often qualifies

Common categories include teachers, first responders, medical professionals, military members, and other community heroes — confirmation depends on current program rules and verification steps.

If you are unsure, ask early. Do not assume you are in or out based only on a job title. See also: [Who qualifies for Homes for Heroes?](/resources/faqs/who-qualifies-homes-for-heroes).

## What the savings are (and aren’t)

**Are:**

- A potential financial benefit tied to a qualifying buy and/or sell
- Something to plan into your overall cash-to-close or net-sheet conversation
- Compatible with working directly with Calvin as your local Realtor

**Are not:**

- A replacement for correct pricing strategy
- A reason to skip inspections
- A guarantee that every property or loan program will behave the same
- An excuse to ignore monthly payment reality

## Timing: bring it up early

Mention Homes for Heroes at the beginning of your buy or sell conversation so:

- Eligibility questions don’t delay closing
- Your net sheet / cash-to-close estimates include the right assumptions
- Marketing and offer strategy stay focused on the property — with savings as an added benefit

## Buying with Hero Rewards in mind

Still do the fundamentals:

1. Get financing clarity  
2. Choose communities on purpose ([Community guides](/community))  
3. Tour with a scorecard  
4. Write offers you can support through appraisal and inspection  

Then let affiliate savings improve the outcome — not define the search.

## Selling as a hero

If you are selling, Hero Rewards may apply to your transaction when you qualify. You still need:

- Honest pricing
- Strong prep and photos
- A clean negotiation plan

Savings do not fix an overpriced listing.
