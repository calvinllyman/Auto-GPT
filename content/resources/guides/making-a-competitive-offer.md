A competitive offer is more than the highest number. It’s the package a seller believes will actually close — on a timeline they can trust.

## What sellers usually weigh

- Purchase price vs. recent comps
- Earnest money and option period length
- Financing strength (pre-approval, down payment, loan type)
- Contingencies and repair expectations
- Closing date and possession needs
- How clean the paperwork looks

## Price with comps, not hope

Ask for recent solds that match the home you’re writing on. Bidding far above weak comps can create appraisal risk; bidding far below a hot listing can end the conversation on day one.

## Terms that often matter

- **Option period:** Enough time to inspect, not so long that the seller feels parked
- **Earnest money:** Signals seriousness when sized appropriately for the price
- **Appraisal gap language:** Only if you understand the cash risk
- **Seller credits:** Useful when payment matters more than sticker price

## Emotion vs. process

Tour with a clear must-have list. Sleep on it when you can. Then write with a plan — not a panic.
