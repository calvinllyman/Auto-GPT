Market reports are useful when you know what the numbers are trying to tell you. This primer helps buyers and sellers interpret common OKC metro stats without overreacting to headlines.

## Inventory

More active listings usually means more choices for buyers and more competition for sellers. Low inventory often means sharper offer strategy and careful pricing.

## Prices

Median prices are a snapshot, not your home’s value. Neighborhood, condition, and updates can place you above or below the median.

## Days on market

Faster sales can signal strong demand — or underpricing. Slower sales can mean overpricing, condition issues, or a seasonal lull.

## Use reports as context, not a verdict

Your decision should combine market stats with your timeline, finances, and goals.
