There isn’t one perfect month to buy or sell in the Oklahoma City metro — but seasons do change the feel of the market. Use this as a timing guide, then match it to your life (job start, school year, lease end).

## Spring

Often busier for listings and showings. Buyers may face more competition; sellers who prep early can catch stronger traffic. Landscaping and curb appeal start to matter more in photos and first impressions.

## Summer

Families still move, but heat and vacations can shape showing schedules. Well-cooled homes and flexible showing windows help. Inventory patterns vary year to year — don’t assume summer is “slow” without checking your price band.

## Fall

A practical window for motivated buyers and sellers who want to close before the holidays. Presentation still matters; shorter daylight can make lighting and evening curb appeal more important.

## Winter

Fewer casual lookers in many years — which can mean a more serious buyer pool for well-priced homes. Sellers may trade volume of showings for clarity of interest. Buyers sometimes find more room to negotiate, depending on inventory.

## Choose timing for *your* goal

- Need maximum buyer traffic? Lean into peak showing seasons with strong prep.
- Need certainty on a date? Price and condition matter more than the calendar.
- Buying with a school-year deadline? Start financing and search earlier than you think.
