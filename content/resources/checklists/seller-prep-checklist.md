Preparation is one of the highest-ROI parts of selling. You don’t need a full remodel — you need a clear plan.

## Start here

- Declutter rooms so photos show space, not storage stress
- Deep clean kitchens, baths, floors, and windows
- Fix obvious small repairs buyers will notice immediately
- Neutralize heavy personal décor where it helps buyers imagine living there

## Outside matters

- Mow, trim, and clear the entry path
- Clean the front door area — first impressions start at the curb
- Check exterior lights and house numbers

## Before you price

Get a local valuation conversation so prep decisions match market expectations — not Pinterest alone.
