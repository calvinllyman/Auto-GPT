Prep is how you protect price. You do not need a full remodel — you need a home that photographs well, shows clean, and does not hand buyers easy inspection ammunition.

## Tier 1 — safety and deal-killers

- Fix active leaks and obvious electrical hazards
- Ensure smoke/CO detectors work
- Address broken rails, missing GFCI where expected, and unsafe steps
- Make sure HVAC heats/cools for showing season
- Resolve pest activity you know about

## Tier 2 — first-impression ROI

- Deep clean (kitchen grease, bathrooms, baseboards, windows)
- Declutter counters, closets, and garage enough for photos
- Fresh neutral paint on the worst walls/rooms
- Consistent lighting (replace mixed/burned-out bulbs)
- Mow, edge, trim, and clear the entry

Use the [listing photo-ready checklist](/resources/checklists/listing-photo-ready-checklist) the day before photography.

## Tier 3 — smart repairs (usually)

- Regrout/recaulk moldy wet areas
- Fix loud plumbing issues and running toilets
- Repair damaged flooring transitions buyers trip on visually
- Service HVAC and replace filter before showings
- Touch up exterior trim/doors that look tired in photos

## Tier 4 — usually skip before listing

- Luxury kitchen remodels that won’t return dollar-for-dollar in your price band
- Personal design experiments that narrow the buyer pool
- Incomplete projects that look abandoned mid-stream

When in doubt, price the home honestly instead of over-improving.

## Paperwork and logistics

- Gather keys, remotes, codes, warranties, and HOA docs if applicable
- List known repairs/improvements with approximate dates
- Plan showing access (lockbox, pets, kids’ schedules)
- Decide possession timing before you negotiate it under pressure
