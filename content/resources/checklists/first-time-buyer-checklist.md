Buying your first home goes smoother when you know the sequence. Use this checklist as a working list — not a perfection test.

## Before you tour

- Talk with a lender and get a pre-approval letter
- Set a realistic monthly payment range (not just a purchase price)
- List must-haves vs. nice-to-haves for the OKC metro lifestyle you want
- Decide which communities you’re willing to compare (Yukon, Mustang, OKC, and beyond)

## While you search

- Tour with purpose — take notes on light, noise, storage, and commute
- Ask about HOAs, flood history, and recent repairs early
- Keep emotion in check until inspection findings are in

## Offer through closing

- Understand earnest money, option periods, and contingencies
- Budget for closing costs, moving, and immediate setup expenses
- Confirm insurance, utilities, and final walkthrough timing
