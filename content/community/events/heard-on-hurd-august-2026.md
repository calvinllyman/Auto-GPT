**Heard on Hurd** is Edmond’s signature monthly street festival — food trucks, pop-up shops, and live local music every third Saturday from March through October.

## When and where

- **Saturday, August 15, 2026**
- **6:00 PM – 10:00 PM**
- **Downtown Edmond**

## What to expect

- Dozens of food trucks and local vendors
- Live music from local artists
- A walkable downtown block-party feel
- Free parking throughout much of downtown Edmond (arrive early on busy nights)

## Neighborhood angle

If you’re comparing Edmond to Yukon or north OKC, Heard on Hurd is a useful “Saturday night test” — loud, social, and very downtown. Some buyers want to be close to it; others prefer quieter pockets a short drive away.
