Wrap up summer with **Kimbell Bay’s Back to School Bash** — a free, family-focused night from Yukon Parks & Recreation.

## When and where

- **Friday, August 7, 2026**
- **6:00 PM – 11:00 PM**
- **Kimbell Park, Yukon**

## Planned highlights

- Games, music, and water tag
- Night swim
- Food truck on site
- Outdoor movie beginning around **9:00 PM** (*SpongeBob SquarePants: Search for SquarePants*, per city listing)

No registration required for the bash itself (confirm any pool rules on arrival).

## Why it matters if you’re house-hunting

Park-centered events are a practical lifestyle signal: where families gather after work, how busy evenings feel, and what “community” looks like beyond a listing photo.
