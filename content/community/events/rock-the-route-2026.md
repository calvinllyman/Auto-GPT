Celebrate the **100th anniversary of Route 66** at Rock the Route — one of Yukon’s biggest free community nights, right where the Mother Road runs through downtown.

## When and where

- **Thursday, September 3, 2026**
- **6:00 PM – 11:00 PM**
- **Downtown Yukon on Route 66** (around 5th & Main Street)

## Planned evening flow

- **6:00 PM** — Kids area and food trucks open
- **7:00 PM** — Smokin’ Oaks
- **9:00 PM** — Josh Weathers
- **10:30 PM** — Drone show and fireworks
- **11:00 PM** — Event ends

## Good to know

Admission is free. The City asks that attendees respect road closures, skip unauthorized drones, and use free public parking around the event perimeter without blocking driveways.

## Neighborhood angle

If you’re comparing Yukon to other west-metro cities, nights like Rock the Route show how downtown and Route 66 still function as a real gathering place — not just a driving corridor.

Details can change; confirm on the [City of Yukon Special Events page](https://www.cityofyukonok.gov/273/Special-Events).
