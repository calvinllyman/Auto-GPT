**Cruise-in for a Cause** returns to downtown Yukon for an evening of classic cars, food, music, and community fundraising along historic Route 66.

## When and where

- **Thursday, September 17, 2026**
- **5:00 PM – 9:00 PM**
- **Main Street, Downtown Yukon**

## What to expect

- Park-and-show classic / special-interest vehicles (limited show spots — register early if you’re bringing a car)
- Food, music, and family-friendly downtown energy
- A Route 66 centennial celebration angle for 2026
- Fundraiser support for the **Yukon Veterans Museum**

## Why locals show up

Main Street events are one of the easiest ways to feel Yukon’s downtown rhythm — especially if you’re deciding whether “west metro” means quiet streets, active downtown nights, or both.

Confirm registration and details through [Yukon 66 Main Street](https://yukon66mainstreet.com/).
